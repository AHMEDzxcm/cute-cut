ffmpeg-core.js is the unmodified upstream UMD loader.
ffmpeg-core.bin is the unmodified upstream ffmpeg-core.wasm compressed with gzip.
Decompress it with standard gzip tools to recover the original binary.

The core includes codecs and libraries listed by FFmpeg's -version output.
The wrapper's source maps are not needed at runtime.
